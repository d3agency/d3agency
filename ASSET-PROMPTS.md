# Generated website assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded as WebP for the website.

- `public/cloud-sky.webp`: Wide dreamy daylight sky with recognizable white cumulus cloud banks at the lower sides, pale blue and cream light, clear central space for a dark headline. Natural photographic softness and fine grain. No text, land, buildings, or graphics.
- `public/ascii-town.webp`: Original panoramic ASCII-style Mediterranean hilltop town: recognizable cathedral dome, bell tower, tiled-roof houses, arched colonnades, winding stairs and distant hills. Tiny monospaced character-like marks in dark warm gray on white. Clear architecture and continuous ground. No readable text or frame. Oscar Moxon's footer artwork supplied as a style reference.

Original generated PNG files remain in the local Codex generated-images directory; these optimized copies are included in the project and deployment.
