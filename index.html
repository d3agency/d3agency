04 — BEFORE WE START

Questions,
answered.

What does D3 Agency do?

D3 Agency is an independent studio run by Dhruv. It designs and builds custom websites, web apps and MVPs, and automates the work around them, such as enquiry forms connected to a CRM and follow-up workflows.

Who is D3 Agency for?

Founders with a product idea they want to launch, and businesses that need a better website or want to stop doing the same admin by hand. If you can describe the problem, that is enough to start.

What can you automate on my website?

Common examples are enquiry forms that send leads straight to a CRM, instant replies and follow-up emails, notifications to your team, and syncing data between the tools you already use. We also build D3CRM, our own CRM for website enquiries.

How does a project work?

It starts with a conversation about what you want to build. Then comes a clear scope, design and development with regular check-ins, and launch. You work directly with Dhruv the whole way, with no account managers in between.

How much does a website or web app cost?

Pricing and timelines depend on scope, so they are agreed once we understand what you want to build. Send a message with your idea and you will get a clear estimate before any work starts.

Is D3 Agency the same as D3 Tech?

Yes. D3 Agency, D3 Tech and d3tech.agency are the same studio, run by Dhruv.
